FundsXML Training Videos

Three series about FundsXML, the open standard for fund data exchange. Each film is marked with its audience: business readers, data specialists who work with the files, or engineers who build the systems. All examples use fictional funds and synthetic identifiers. The narration is AI-generated (a synthetic voice, texts reviewed by people); the picture shows the real application.
