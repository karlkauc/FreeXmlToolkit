    Industry Extensions¶

    FreeXmlToolkit ships optional extensions for industry standards. Each one has its own page that explains its functions in detail:

    Extension For Page
    ISO 20022 Payments (SEPA) pain, pacs and camt payment messages; control sums and the EPC SEPA rules ISO 20022 Payments
    ebInterface Austrian XML e-invoices 4.1–6.1; line, tax and total calculations ebInterface
    FinDatEx Templates EMT, EET, EPT and TPT (V7/V8) files as CSV or Excel FinDatEx Templates
    XBRL (with Arelle) XBRL instances and inline XBRL reports, validated with Arelle XBRL
    PK-Meldewesen (FMA) Austrian pension fund reports to the FMA: quarterly (PK-QMV), annual (PK-FJMV) and ECB report PK-Meldewesen
    ESMA Reporting (EMIR, SFTR, MiFIR) EMIR Refit trade reports (auth.030), SFTR reports (auth.052) and MiFIR transaction reports (auth.016) with ESMA's validation rules ESMA Reporting
    KAG-Meldewesen (FMA) Reports of Austrian management companies to the FMA: AIFMD reporting (AIFM and AIF file) with the FMA's rules, money market fund report, derivative risk report (4. DeRiMV) KAG-Meldewesen

    The FundsXML extension is an extension as well, with its own page.

    Switching them on¶

    The industry extensions are off by default: switch them on in Settings → Extensions → INSTALLED EXTENSIONS and restart. When you open a document that a switched-off extension recognises (a SEPA payment, an e-invoice, a FinDatEx template, an XBRL instance, an ESMA or a KAG report), a bar above the editor offers it once: Switch On stores the setting, the extension is there after the next start. Administrators can keep an extension off for everyone with plugin.<id>.enabled=false (iso20022, ebinterface, findatex, xbrl, kag-meldewesen, esma-reporting), see Administration.

    What every industry extension adds¶

    • an activity in the Activity Bar with a side panel:
      • ABOUT — what the extension checks;
      • ACTIVE DOCUMENT — what the open document is (message type, invoice number, template, number of facts …), updated when you switch tabs;
      • Check Active Document — validates the document like F8 (schema and the extension's rules); the problems go to the PROBLEMS panel below the editor, where a click jumps to the line, and are underlined in the text. The side panel shows how many errors and warnings the last check found; Show in Problems opens the list. FinDatEx and XBRL run their own check (XBRL needs the saved file) and put their problems into the PROBLEMS panel the same way;
      • Check Report (HTML) (ESMA Reporting, KAG-Meldewesen) — the findings grouped by rule with the field they belong to, as a page in the preview;
      • TOOLS — the extension's own actions (sample documents, new template files, Excel import, Arelle command);
      • OPTIONS (KAG-Meldewesen) — the extension's settings;
      • CHECK AN IDENTIFIER — checks a pasted IBAN (ISO 13616: country length and check digits), BIC (ISO 9362), LEI (ISO 17442), ISIN (ISO 6166) or Austrian VAT id (UID), depending on the extension; an LEI and an ISIN can be looked up online, and Check Identifiers Online checks every LEI (GLEIF) and ISIN (ESMA FIRDS, OpenFIGI) of the document (Online Identifier Lookup);
    • a progress block under the actions while a check, report or tool runs: a bar (with a count where the work can be counted - records of a KAG/ESMA report, requests of the online identifier check), what runs right now, the time so far and Cancel where the work can stop (between records, requests or steps; Arelle is ended at once). A second click on a running action is refused; with several actions at once the newest is shown with "(+1 more)";
    • checks that run with every validation (Validation panel, F8, fxt-cli validate) and appear with the extension's name as source — ISO 20022 and ebInterface; FinDatEx and XBRL files are checked from their side panel;
    • where the standard has official XML Schemas, Schema Library entries (source Extension), so documents get their schema bound automatically. Remote schemas are downloaded once and cached;
    • for the reports with field catalogues (ESMA Reporting, KAG-Meldewesen): the field number, name and rule of an element in the Inspector, as tooltip in the grid and - when the mouse rests on a tag name - in the text editor (documents up to 2 MB).

    ebInterface versions 4.1–6.1 yes ebInterface 6.1 invoice UID, IBAN, BIC
